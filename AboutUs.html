About Us

ClinTrack is an educational portal developed and published by 2nd year nursing students of Sacred Heart College as a requirement in Nursing Informatics. It serves as a centralized platform for compiling and organizing clinical rotation information, ensuring efficient documentation and student compliance throughout their training.

Our Mission

At ClinTrack, our mission is to enhance clinical learning by providing a comprehensive digital platform that streamlines the management of clinical schedules, requirements, and records for nursing students. We are dedicated to promoting organization, accuracy, and accountability through accessible and efficient documentation. By bridging academic requirements and clinical practice, we aim to support students in effectively managing their duties and becoming competent future nurses.

Our Vision

ClinTrack aims to become a user-friendly and innovative clinical documentation system that enhances nursing education by streamlining clinical records management and fostering competence, organization, and excellence among student nurses.
